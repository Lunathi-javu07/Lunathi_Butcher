Our Story

About Lunathi's Butcher

Our History

Welcome to Lunathi's Butcher. The business has been operating since 2021 and has grown to become a trusted name in the community, known for our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.

Based in Zwide, Gqeberha, we have been a fast-growing business in the area because we offer quality meat to the locals. Our team is made up of experienced local butchers who are passionate about their craft and dedicated to providing the best possible service.

We believe in honesty, transparency, and building strong relationships with our customers and the wider Zwide community. We source our meat from trusted and reliable suppliers, and we are always happy to answer any questions about our products and services.

Goals & Commitment

The goal of Lunathi's Butcher is to create more jobs in the community and teach people useful skills they can apply to start their own businesses. We are committed to maintaining our reputation for producing high-quality products for our customers.

We are dedicated to environmental responsibility and sustainability, reducing our impact while promoting eco-friendly practices across everything we do.

  • Community job creation
  • Skills development programs
  • High-quality local sourcing
  • Environmental responsibility
