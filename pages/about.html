About Us

History

Welcome to Lunathi's Butcher.The business has been operating since 2021. Lunathi's Butcher has grown to become a trusted name in the community, known for our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction. The business is based in Zwide, Gqeberha and it has been a fast-growing business in the area because it offers quality meat to the locals.The business is committed to providing high-quality products and services to its customers. We take pride in our work and strive to exceed our customer's expactactions. Our team is made up of experienced local butchers who are passionate about their job and dedication to providing the best possible service to our customers. We believe in building strong relationships with our customers and community, we are committed to supporting local farmers and suppliers, and we source our meat from trusted and reliable sources. We believe in honesty and transparency in our business practises, and we are always happy to answer any questions our customers may have about our products and services.Thank you for choosing Lunathi's Butcher, and we look forward to serving you.

Goals and commitment

The goal of Lunathi's Butcher is to create more jobs in the community through the business and teach people of the community about useful skills they can apply to start their own businesses. The business is commited to maintaining its reputation for producing high-quality to its customers.
